## Serialization and terminal evidence

Bluetooth and audio use the same host-global guard in
`tools/hardware/device-trial-guard.py`. It takes one nonblocking cross-process
lock under `/home/corpunum/.local/state/s22-device-trial-guard`, validates
private file/directory identity, and fsyncs a one-shot `pending` marker before
the first remote write. A crash or timeout leaves pending/UNKNOWN state that
blocks every subsequent device operation. Successful receipts can terminalize
the marker; reconciliation is explicit and preserves the old marker. The
guard never clears a marker or retries a trial ID.

The adapter can mark the trial complete only after the private board receipt
and raw trace have been fsynced and checked. It requires same-boot healthy
before/after status; after-metadata with no device FDs and USB health; the
exact WLAN vote check; HCI registration and successful bridge output; one
successful TTY detach; the explicit `power_off_and_vote_restored` stage; a
nonempty trace at the reserved path whose bytes match the recorded SHA-256;
and an empty/fatal-free kernel delta. It then independently re-reads the
candidate snapshot, full RECOVERY hash, network/native/model/power health, and
controller absence on that same boot, persists the result into the receipt,
and only then terminalizes the marker. Any missing, false, or unreadable field
leaves the operation UNKNOWN.

## Kernel behavior and stop conditions

The existing verified initialization ordering is unchanged. Successful
`HCIUARTSETPROTO` registers the HCI device, and the pinned kernel queues
automatic initialization/power-on; setup may initialize the controller and
set `HCI_UP`. The procedure issues no explicit `HCIDEVUP`, scan, advertising,
connection, or pairing request, but this is not a zero-controller-activation
or zero-RF guarantee. Automatic initialization is part of the separately
authorized trial.

The userspace bridge loop is 20 seconds; that does not bound the kernel's
`TIOCSETD` detach. Line-discipline teardown synchronously waits for kernel
work cancellation and core/transport close, with no internal overall deadline.
A host timeout or disconnect is UNKNOWN—not proof of detach, Bluetooth
power-off, or safe recovery. No retry/reset/reconciliation is automatic. On
any error or unknown outcome, stop and preserve evidence; never repeat the
consumed raw-HCI socket operation. Registration does not establish radio,
pairing, or Bluetooth-audio acceptance.
