## Bridge lifecycle change

The embedded bridge previously opened a second `AF_BLUETOOTH` raw socket after
its 20-second bridge interval to print adapter statistics. It did so even when
the bridge had failed or received a stop signal. The bridge no longer opens
that socket. A successful `HCIUARTGETDEVICE` now supplies the registration
receipt (`bridge_registered_hci=<index>`); in the pinned
`drivers/bluetooth/hci_ldisc.c`, this ioctl returns `hu->hdev->id` only when
`HCI_UART_REGISTERED` is set, and otherwise returns `-EUNATCH`.

The bridge detaches the temporary `N_HCI` line discipline through its single
cleanup path. Failure to obtain the HCI device index after protocol setup also
reaches that path. This avoids a repeated raw-socket operation and keeps
failure cleanup limited to detaching the temporary HCI UART instance. It does
not turn a host registration receipt into phone or controller acceptance.

The runner's `--execute` guard remains closed. Its message now reflects the
consumed one-shot socket check and requires a fresh exact RECOVERY identity and
full readback, independent review of attach/detach/poweroff, and separate
explicit authorization. Its configured binary and staging destination point
to this milestone's unique paths.
